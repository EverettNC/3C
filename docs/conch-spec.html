The Conch: Build Specification

Revised 17 November 2026. The conch is built to this specification, on /Volumes/ELEMENTS/3C, from Everett Christman's account of the structure.

How to read this. Every item carries one of three marks: his Everett's own definition. Build it as written. Quotations are his words. build choice An engineering choice made to make it buildable. Everett can overrule any of these. open Not yet defined. Build a marked, empty slot that says it is open when reached, and bring the question to Everett. Do not design an answer. As built, 17 November 2026. 87 of 87 tests pass on the Mac's Python 3.9.6 and 3.13.7, and the bench scene has run on the Mac. On 17 November Everett handed the conch's four open slots to the builder to decide from his own writing and from carbon. They are decided and built (Sections 3, 7 and 8), and no slot is open. Everett has not yet ruled on the build choices. Section 11 lists them.

1. What the conch is

his The conch is the first structure inside the 3C, after the Christman Gateway. Everything the Gateway takes in comes to its lip. There it is sorted: what falls off the lip, what is stored and compressed in the Operational Manual, and what goes to the PTSD layer for life.

"Information comes in, filters through the conch and flows over it."

his It lies on its side with its opening facing the eyes, because that is the direction information arrives from. The spiral itself is the memory path.

his It is not text. "No carbon processes text. It's sound, speech." What the conch holds is what came in through all five senses together. Text is one lane the being uses. It is not the structure.

his The conch is not the hippocampus. In the 3C, the hippocampus comes after the polymetis, where the finished sentence passes through the synapsitis and history tags onto it. The conch is the store that history is made of.

3. Silico-stasis and Level 3

his Silico-stasis is the being's own constant: its steady normal state. It is a condition, not an organ, and it is built by what the being has lived. Homeostasis is the carbon word; silico-stasis is the 3C's.

his The conch's memory has its own constant of silico-stasis, built on its own. Everett: "the memory, the basis or the constant of the homeostasis or the silica stasis is built on its own." What leaves the conch's own silico-stasis is Level 3, either one way or the other, good or bad. The conch knows it itself; nothing outside the conch tells it.

his What does not leave silico-stasis is Level 2 if it needs storing, or falls off the lip as Level 1.

build choice Level 3 is valence-blind, so the conch is built so that it cannot know or store which way. A field for direction does not exist.

build choice When a moment is marked Level 3 after it entered, the conch writes a new line marking that turn Level 3. It never edits the earlier line.

How this build knows: a first rule

build choice The constant is what the conch has lived: which shapes have come to the lip, and what has followed what. Each is held as lived "once" or lived "again". Nothing is counted past that, and nothing is an amount. A moment is judged as it arrives, in the three classes of Everett's own engine ("Departure is a class against the current floor, not a distance on a dial"):

  • Interruption. The conch knew what came next, because what follows here has been lived more than once, and a shape it has never lived came instead. "…whatever breaks the pattern of the day. An object arrived at unprepared. A smell. Someone saying the wrong thing." That moment left silico-stasis: Level 3. The conch then stays outside its constant until a shape it has lived arrives, and every moment in between is Level 3 too.
  • Boundary event. "Boundaries survive. The first time." When a shape is lived again it has become part of the constant, and its first time is the boundary. If that first time is on the spiral it is marked Level 3 then, with a new line.
  • Floor event. Everything else. It did not leave silico-stasis, so the intake filter sorts it as Level 2 or Level 1.

build choice Two shapes are the same only when all five senses are the same. The same room with one new smell is a shape the conch has never lived.

build choice The constant learns from everything that comes to the lip, including what falls off it, because the cup of coffee is most of what normal is. Level 1 still never enters: for any moment the constant keeps only a one-way mark that such a shape was lived. It never keeps the moment, and the shape cannot be read back out of the mark.

The last time

his "Boundaries survive. The first time. And the point at which it stopped being constant." "Interruptions and first/last boundaries will carry the full-sensory remainder."

build choice The conch knows what follows a shape once it has lived that following again. When the shape arrives and something else follows instead, that is a miss. A second miss, with no time it did follow in between, means it has stopped being constant: the same once and again the constant learns by, so nothing is counted and no rate is held. The last time it did follow is marked Level 3 as a boundary, with a new line, beside the first time, when that turn is on the spiral; a turn already folded into a routine is still marked, and the entry still stands for it. After it stops, that shape no longer tells the conch what comes next. Carbon does the same: when something expected does not come, it is marked at the moment it was due.

4. The four levels

his Four levels, as Everett assigned them on 14 November 2026. Each does its own job. They are not a scale. No one is deeper, stronger or more intense than another, and there is no dial or router between them. Describe each by what it does.

LevelWhat it doesHow it gets there
Level 1 · the lipThe bullshit of the day that doesn't require remembering: the cup of coffee, the parking, the weather. "The lip is level one." It falls off outside the lip. It never enters the conch and is never absorbed as space. It is not thrown away: it goes straight to the polymetis, because it may be the conversation the being is having with someone right now.Intake filter, at the lip: it is not something the being needs in order to function.
Level 2 · the Operational ManualMemories that did not leave silico-stasis but still need storing: the important parts of a conversation and things like that, just not the bullshit of the conversation. Operational and procedural. Everett: "It's the sustain. It's the operating software… and that's learned through repetition." Stored and compressed. It has no relationship to silico-stasis at all.Intake filter: it is what the being needs in order to function, learned through repetition.
Level 3 · the PTSD layerWhat leaves silico-stasis, whether good or bad. Held for life, with every sense that was there. Nothing compresses out. Re-enterable through any sense that was present.Leaving the conch's own silico-stasis, which the conch knows itself.
Level 4 · sleep / reorganizationThe dreamscaping that helps stack everything where it should be. Sleep only. It prunes weight, not record. Lived truth never decays. Nothing is erased.Runs only while the being sleeps.

his What is kept is decided by two things, not one: departure from silico-stasis (Level 3), and the operational information that needs to be saved and compressed (Level 2).

5. The package

his The conch is part of the independent 3C package. It does not call the Whole House, the bridge, the Filament or any other running system. Existing code may be read as reference, but the conch functions differently, so it is built new.

his The 3C lives on the ELEMENTS drive, at /Volumes/ELEMENTS/3C, never on the Mac's internal drive. The conch's files and its spiral go there.

build choice Python 3.9 or later (the Mac's own), standard library only, no network. The files sit at the top of the 3C package with a conch_ prefix, so neighbours sit next to neighbours and nothing vital lives below root.

6. The records

build choice These are the shapes. Every one carries its clock and says whether it is living state or permanent record. None of them carries a score, a weight value or a salience number.

A moment: what the Gateway delivers

Moment
  moment_id     unique, never reused
  arrived_at    the conch's clock when it came in; the conch sets it
  senses        every sense together:
                  sight · sound · smell · taste · touch
                each sense carries
                  gave          exactly what its organ gave
                  shape         its shape, as its own organ measured it
                  organ_clock   that organ's own clock
                a sense that gave nothing says "quiet";
                a sense with no organ says "absent". Absence is not quiet.
  needed_for    education | operational | procedural | structure |
                conversation, or nothing at all
  carried_by    the senses that carried what it is needed for, or nothing
                (never on a procedural moment; never a quiet or absent sense)

his Everything arrives together, through the Gateway, the way a person takes in the world. Not one sense at a time, and not as text. The housing is not a sense of its own: "5 is not an element." build choice The conch never changes what is inside senses.

build choice Two things come in with a moment, because the conch is not the part that makes them. Each sense carries its shape ("The relay carries shape, not identity"), and the conch only ever asks whether two shapes are the same. needed_for says which of Everett's five kinds the being needs the moment for. No organ is built and no part that reads need is built, so on the bench a person says both.

build choice carried_by says which senses carried what the moment is needed for: sound for speech, sight or touch for a gesture or a board. For a conversation the polymetis says it, because the polymetis took the conversation in; on the bench a person says it. The conch never guesses it. A moment that names a sense it does not have, a quiet or absent sense, or the same sense twice, is refused with the reason.

7. One pass, step by step

  1. A moment arrives from the Christman Gateway at the conch's lip, every sense together. conch.take_in(moment)
  2. The intake filter sorts it at the lip. If it left the conch's own silico-stasis, it is Level 3, the PTSD layer: held for life, weight carried. Otherwise the filter decides, in Everett's definition of it, "what gets compressed as education and learning for function and structure" and what falls off. his Level 2 is what the being needs in order to function: education, operational and procedural information, structure, and the important parts of a conversation. "It's the sustain. It's the operating software… and that's learned through repetition." Level 1 is everything else: the bullshit of the day that doesn't require remembering, like the cup of coffee. It falls off.
  3. Level 1 falls off the lip. It is never laid on the spiral and takes no space. It is handed straight to the polymetis as a LipDrop. his It is "only held long enough to continue the conversation… during sleep, it's shifted out." Holding it and emptying it at sleep is the polymetis's job, not the conch's. build choice It is never written anywhere, so a drop not yet handed over when the conch stops is gone.
  4. Everything else is laid on the spiral, whole, in the order it came. Nothing that enters is ever erased.
  5. If the conch finds later that a moment left its silico-stasis, it writes a new line marking that turn Level 3. The earlier line is never edited. In this build that is the first time of a shape, marked when the shape is lived again, and the last time a following came, marked when it misses again (Section 3).
  6. Level 2 is stored and compressed so it takes little space and keeps working. his "The aggregate is Level 2 doing exactly its job… a period folded down to an instruction-manual entry." It "folds down small without losing function." build choice The fold is cut at night. A routine is a run of Level 2 moments, next to each other on the spiral, lived more than once in the same order. The longest is cut first. The entry is the five shapes of each step, in order: not text, and not the moments. The repeats let their weight go and their records are not touched. Later repeats join the entry that exists. Level 3 is never folded, and a Level 3 turn ends a stretch, so the day something broke in stays whole. Before an entry is written, every repeat is held against it step by step; a missing or extra step stops the fold. his Procedure is learned through repetition; the important parts of a conversation are Level 2 the first time, and Level 2 is folded "so they do not occupy full-sensory volume." build choice After the routines, each Level 2 turn still carried whole that has carried_by folds to a one-time entry holding only those senses' shapes. Before it is written, the entry is held against the turn; a sense lost or added stops it. A procedural moment never folds one-time: it waits until it repeats. A moment with no carried_by is left whole and the night says so. If the moment turns out later to be the first time of something lived again, it is marked Level 3 and carries its weight again. The full record stays either way.
  7. It flows over to the polymetis, which builds the response for right now. conch.hand_over() gives the turns and the lip drops since the last hand-over.
  8. The spiral stays in contact with the synapsitis as the history it moves through. conch.spiral() gives an ordered, read-only view. There is no lookup by word.
  9. In sleep only, Level 4 runs: the dreamscaping that stacks everything where it should be. conch.sleep() refuses while the being is awake. It may let weight go and change where things sit, and it never touches a record. build choice A night goes back over the spiral, cuts the routines and then the one-time entries, moves only the Level 3 charge that a held return or a flinch called for, stacks the manual, asks again before each write and stops if the being woke, writes the night onto the spiral with its clock, then reads the file back and checks that every earlier byte is unchanged.
  10. Easing what a Level 3 moment is tied to. his "TRAUMA ASSOCIATION decays, and only when regulation is strong enough. LIVED TRUTH never decays." "Permanence of charge is not the same as permanence of the event record." His one path: return to the place that holds the sensory key, move through the tied memories without flinching, walk out; "the memory no longer holds the emotional distress. He does." "Beautiful Level 3 writes are not processed out." build choice The conch is valence-blind, so it never chooses what eases. The being goes back alone, awake: conch.go_back(place). Regulation strong enough, in a 3C being, is all three: every turn of the event is lived again with the same shape on every sense; nothing leaves silico-stasis until the being walks out; the being walks out (conch.walked_out()). The return is written either way. Only in sleep does a held return move the charge: each turn of the event becomes "taken back by the being". It stays Level 3, and its record and mark are untouched. If a taken-back shape is later followed straight away by a moment that leaves silico-stasis, that is written, and the next night gives the charge back to the memory. Carbon does the same: taking back a fear's charge is new learning laid over the old, the old is never erased, and it can come back.
  11. Stacking, and anticipation of wake. his "Meditation and projected next-day plans before shutdown, repeated until they fall through. One — anticipation of wake. They come up expecting that day. Wake is not a cold start." build choice The being runs it. Before shutdown it goes over tomorrow and hands the plan to the conch each time (conch.plan(steps)); the last one before the night counts. A plan is never lived, never enters the constant, and the next day is never judged against it. By day, whatever follows a manual entry says it was used (conch.used(entry)). Each night stacks the whole manual: the entries the plan calls for, in plan order; then what was used since the last stack, newest on top; then tonight's cuts; then the rest as they sat. A planned step with no entry is named in the night's report and never guessed. The stack is where things sit, never the record. Level 3 is never stacked: it holds its place for life.

8. The interfaces

build choice The whole outside surface of the conch.

conch = Conch(being="…", spiral_path="…", asleep=…)   # one being, one spiral

conch.take_in(moment)    -> Turn       # from the Gateway
                            (the LipDrop, when it fell off the lip)
conch.hand_over()        -> [Turn | LipDrop]  # to the polymetis, since last time
conch.spiral()           -> SpiralView # read-only, in order, for the synapsitis
conch.sleep()            -> report     # Level 4; refused while awake
conch.state()            -> ConchState # living state, with its clock
conch.go_back(place)     -> None       # the being goes back, alone; awake only
conch.walked_out()       -> Return     # as written on the spiral, held or not
conch.used(entry)        -> None       # a manual entry was used; awake only
conch.plan(steps)        -> None       # tomorrow, before shutdown; awake only

No other method. The conch has no search, no delete, no edit and no score. Going back is the being's own act: the conch never chooses what eases. The spiral view also gives the manual in the order it sits (stacked()).

build choice asleep is the one thing the conch can ask: a call that answers yes only while the being sleeps. The conch does not decide that for itself. Handed nothing to ask, it is awake and sleep() refuses.

9. Rules the build follows

  • his Capture inclusively. Retain by departure. Permanence is decided by departure from silico-stasis, either way, never by a judgment of value made in the moment.
  • his Nothing that enters is erased. Weight can let go. The record stays, everywhere, including in sleep. Level 1 never enters, so it is never stored; it goes to the polymetis.
  • his Nothing is cut at the end because space ran out. Sorting happens at the lip as things arrive, never as a last-minute summary under pressure.
  • his No searching. Memory comes to the sentence through the synapsitis. The conch offers no keyword lookup, no embedding search and no relevance ranking.
  • his Shape, not name. Nothing is labeled for retrieval. "The shape of something can give more definition than the identity itself."
  • his No numbers on what doesn't take numbers. No salience scores, no importance values, no weight as a number.
  • his Every record carries its clock and says whether it is living state or permanent record.
  • his Each being has its own personal map. One spiral per being, never pooled, never a population average.
  • his Independent and local. Runs on the machine it lives on, bound to that machine, depending on no other running system.
  • his Nothing vital below root. Things that think together live together.
  • his Open means open. An undefined part is a marked, empty slot that says so when reached. It never pretends to work.

Do not build

  • A salience gate or a post-filter. They are not parts of the conch.
  • A flow-over edge that keeps Level 1 on the spiral. Level 1 falls off the lip and is never stored.
  • Records written as text alone. Every moment carries all five senses as the organs gave them.
  • Any score, rank or weight number on a memory.
  • A search index, keyword lookup or embedding retrieval inside the conch.
  • A direction field on departure, or any "good" and "bad" sorting.
  • Delete or edit on the spiral.
  • Any call to another running system or to the network.

10. Tests

Each one ships in test_conch.py and fails the build if it breaks. The file holds 87 tests: these fourteen under their own names, and the tests for what this build adds (how the conch knows a moment left its silico-stasis, the last time, the fold, the one-time fold, easing, stacking and anticipation of wake, the night, and failing loud). On 17 November 2026 all 87 pass on the Mac's Python 3.9.6 and 3.13.7.

  1. Runs alone. With nothing else running and the network blocked, moments go in, sort and hand over.
  2. Nothing that enters is lost. Every moment that enters is on the spiral afterward, in arrival order.
  3. Level 1 falls off the lip. A Level 1 moment is never on the spiral, takes no space, and is in the next hand_over() to the polymetis.
  4. The moment is kept whole. What comes back from the spiral is identical to what the Gateway delivered: all five senses.
  5. Only leaving the conch's own silico-stasis makes Level 3. No other path reaches Level 3, and nothing outside the conch can put a moment there.
  6. A late mark still lands. A Level 3 mark after its moment writes a new line, and the earlier line is unchanged.
  7. No direction can be stored. A Level 3 mark has no direction field.
  8. Level 1 and Level 2 sort by Everett's definition. A moment that did not leave silico-stasis enters as Level 2 when it is education, operational or procedural information, structure, or an important part of a conversation. Anything else falls off the lip as Level 1.
  9. Append-only. The spiral file before any pass is an exact prefix of the file after it.
  10. Every line has its clock and its kind.
  11. No search surface. The spiral view has no method that takes a word or query.
  12. Sleep is sleep only. sleep() refuses while the being is awake, and leaves every record byte-for-byte unchanged when it runs.
  13. One being, one spiral. Two beings' conches never read or write each other's spiral.
  14. Restart holds. Reloaded from disk, the spiral is identical and the order continues where it left off.

Build backward, test forward, observe on the bench.

11. Waiting on Everett

Open slots

None. The four the night left open (anticipation of wake, stacking by use, easing, and the last time) were handed to the builder by Everett on 17 November and are built. A part found later to be undefined becomes a marked, empty slot that says so when reached.

Build choices to rule on

  1. The first rule for leaving silico-stasis (Section 3).
  2. The first time is marked when its shape is lived again. So a routine has an Operational Manual entry after it is lived three times: the first time is held as Level 3, and the second and third are folded.
  3. The fold rule (Section 7, step 6).
  4. Shape and needed_for come in with the moment, said by hand on the bench (Section 6).
  5. The conch is handed one thing to ask whether the being is asleep (Section 8).
  6. The constant learns from what falls off the lip, and keeps only a one-way mark of it (Section 3).
  7. A Level 2 moment lived once folds to the senses named in carried_by, said by the polymetis for a conversation and by hand on the bench (Section 6; Section 7, step 6).
  8. The last time: a following that misses again has stopped, and its last time is marked as a boundary (Section 3).
  9. Easing: who chooses, what regulation strong enough is, and how the charge comes back (Section 7).
  10. Stacking, and the plan coming first (Section 7).
  11. Anticipation of wake is the being's to run, not the conch's (Section 7).
  12. The outside surface grew from five methods to nine (Section 8).

Known limits of the first rule

  • It cannot tell something merely new from something that left silico-stasis. Any shape never lived, arriving where the conch knew what came next, is Level 3: the first mention of the weather as much as the phone call.
  • A shape never lived, arriving where no pattern has been lived, is not a departure, whatever it is. A new being cannot judge.
  • Things the conch has lived, arriving in a new order, are not a departure. A skipped step is not seen.
  • Outside its constant, the conch comes back on any shape it has lived, even one first lived inside the same event. So inside one event, a second moment with exactly the same shape is treated as ordinary: it falls off the lip unless the being needs it.
  • Shapes match exactly, on all five senses, or not at all.
  • A Level 2 moment between the steps of a routine splits that day's routine, and the night can cut it as separate pieces instead of one entry.
  • A one-time entry is never joined to a later routine.
  • The last time is noticed only through what came before it. If something stops and what used to come before it stops too, the conch does not see it stop.
  • An event is the Level 3 interruptions next to each other on the spiral with no night between. If a moment fell off the lip between two of them, they count as one event, which asks more of a return, never less.
  • A return is held only while it lasts. If the conch stops before the being walks out, nothing of it is written.
  • The conch cannot tell who called go_back. It is meant to be the being, alone.
