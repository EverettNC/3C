The Conch

The first structure inside the 3C, after the Christman Gateway. It lies sideways, its lip facing the front.

Built and tested on ELEMENTS, 17 November 2026

  • The lip sorts by Everett's definition. Level 2 is what the being needs in order to function: education, operational and procedural information, structure, the important parts of a conversation. Everything else is Level 1 and falls off.
  • The conch knows what left its silico-stasis. It knew what came next, because it has lived it more than once, and something it has never lived came instead. The first time of what becomes constant is held too.
  • Level 2 folds at night. A routine lived more than once in the same order becomes one Operational Manual entry, the shape of each step in order. The repeats let their weight go; their records stay whole. Level 3 is never folded.
  • Level 4 runs only in sleep. It goes back over the spiral, cuts the fold, and checks every earlier line is unchanged.
