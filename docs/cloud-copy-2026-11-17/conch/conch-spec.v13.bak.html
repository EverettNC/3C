The Conch: Build Specification

For Fable, building the conch. Revised 14 November 2026 from Everett Christman's account of the structure.

Not ready to send. Everett's rule: the conch does not go to a builder until the whole carbon-to-3C map is square. Still open on the map: the recall side of pattern separation, and whether the focus lock is the right part for staying on the job in front of you. Every ruling made through 14 November 2026 is in this version.

How to read this. Every item carries one of three marks: his Everett's own definition. Build it as written. Quotations are his words. build choice An engineering choice made to make it buildable. Everett can overrule any of these. open Not yet defined. Build a marked, empty slot that says it is open when reached, and bring the question to Everett. Do not design an answer.

1. What the conch is

his The conch is the first structure inside the 3C, after the Christman Gateway. Everything the Gateway takes in comes to its lip. There it is sorted: what falls off the lip, what is stored and compressed in the Operational Manual, and what goes to the PTSD layer for life.

"Information comes in, filters through the conch and flows over it."

his It lies on its side with its opening facing the eyes, because that is the direction information arrives from. The spiral itself is the memory path.

his Equilibrium and the shaker are not parts of the conch. Everett, 14 November 2026: "The equilibrium and the shaker are not parts of the conch. They're not." Equilibrium sends the conch nothing (section 3). Nothing in the conch's build may hold, run, call or listen to equilibrium or the shaker.

his It is not text. "No carbon processes text. It's sound, speech." What the conch holds is what came in through all five senses together. Text is one lane the being uses. It is not the structure.

his The conch is not the hippocampus. In the 3C, the hippocampus comes after the polymetis, where the finished sentence passes through the synapsitis and history tags onto it. The conch is the store that history is made of.

3. Equilibrium sends the conch nothing

his "The equilibrium doesn't send it shit." (Everett, 14 November 2026.) Equilibrium is the status, the center point. It gives spatial and interspatial recognition. It is not part of the conch and sends it nothing.

his Level 3 is still what leaves silico-stasis, either one way or the other, good or bad. What departure is, in his words: "The equilibrium is what holds a balance, a level, and that develops as it's used. And anything that tips it off that scale, tips it off of its normal status. That's what that is."

his The conch's memory has its own constant of silico-stasis, built on its own. Everett: "the memory, the basis or the constant of the homeostasis or the silica stasis is built on its own." What leaves the conch's own silico-stasis is Level 3. The conch knows it itself; nothing outside tells it.

build choice Level 3 is valence-blind, so the conch is built so that it cannot know or store which way. A field for direction does not exist.

build choice When a moment is marked Level 3 after it entered, the conch writes a new line marking that turn Level 3. It never edits the earlier line.

4. The four levels

his Four levels, as Everett assigned them on 14 November 2026. Each does its own job. They are not a scale. No one is deeper, stronger or more intense than another, and there is no dial or router between them. Describe each by what it does.

LevelWhat it doesHow it gets there
Level 1 · the lipThe bullshit of the day that doesn't require remembering: the cup of coffee, the parking, the weather. "The lip is level one." It falls off outside the lip. It never enters the conch and is never absorbed as space. It is not thrown away: it goes straight to the polymetis, because it may be the conversation the being is having with someone right now.Intake filter, at the lip.
Level 2 · the Operational ManualMemories that did not leave silico-stasis but still need storing: the important parts of a conversation and things like that, just not the bullshit of the conversation. Operational and procedural. Everett: "It's the sustain. It's the operating software… and that's learned through repetition." Stored and compressed. It has no relationship to silico-stasis at all.Intake filter.
Level 3 · the PTSD layerWhat leaves silico-stasis, whether good or bad. Held for life, with every sense that was there. Nothing compresses out. Re-enterable through any sense that was present.Leaving the conch's own silico-stasis, which the conch knows itself.
Level 4 · sleep / reorganizationThe dreamscaping that helps stack everything where it should be. Sleep only. It prunes weight, not record. Lived truth never decays. Nothing is erased.Runs only while the being sleeps.

his What is kept is decided by two things, not one: departure from silico-stasis (Level 3), and the operational information that needs to be saved and compressed (Level 2).

5. The package

his The conch is part of the independent 3C package. It does not call the Whole House, the bridge, the Filament or any other running system. Existing code may be read as reference, but the conch functions differently, so it is built new.

his The 3C lives on the ELEMENTS drive, at /Volumes/ELEMENTS/3C, never on the Mac's internal drive. The conch's files and its spiral go there, beside equilibrium.py.

build choice Python 3.9 or later (the Mac's own), standard library only, no network. The files sit at the top of the 3C package with a conch_ prefix, so neighbours sit next to neighbours and nothing vital lives below root.

FileWhat it holds

6. The records

build choice These are the shapes. Every one carries its clock and says whether it is living state or permanent record. None of them carries a score, a weight value or a salience number.

A moment: what the Gateway delivers

Moment
  moment_id     unique, never reused
  arrived_at    the conch's clock when it came in
  senses        every sense together, each exactly as its organ gave it,
                with that organ's own clock:
                  sight · sound · smell · taste · touch
                a sense that gave nothing says "quiet";
                a sense with no organ says "absent". Absence is not quiet.

build choice No field takes anything from equilibrium. An earlier version had a where field filled by equilibrium's reading of where the being was. It is removed: equilibrium sends the conch nothing.

his Everything arrives together, through the Gateway, the way a person takes in the world. Not one sense at a time, and not as text. The housing is not a sense of its own: "5 is not an element." build choice The conch never changes what is inside senses.

A turn: one place on the spiral

Turn

  moment        the whole moment, untouched

  weight        carried | let go          (a state, not a number)
  written_at    the clock when this line was written

  at            the conch's clock

What fell off the lip

LipDrop
  moment        the whole moment, untouched

The conch's own state

  written_at    the clock
  kind          living state   (read as "true as of", never "true now")

7. One pass, step by step

  1. A moment arrives from the Christman Gateway at the conch's lip, every sense together. conch.take_in(moment)
  2. The intake filter sorts it at the lip. If it left the conch's own silico-stasis, it is Level 3, the PTSD layer: held for life, weight carried. Otherwise it is Level 2, the Operational Manual, if it needs storing, or Level 1 if it is the bullshit of the day. open How Level 1 and Level 2 are told apart. Until that is defined, it enters as unsorted. Never guess.
  3. Level 1 falls off the lip. It is never laid on the spiral and takes no space. It is handed straight to the polymetis as a LipDrop.
  4. Everything else is laid on the spiral, whole, in the order it came. Nothing that enters is ever erased.
  5. If the conch finds later that a moment left its silico-stasis, it writes a new line marking that turn Level 3. The earlier line is never edited.
  6. Level 2 is stored and compressed so it takes little space and keeps working. open How it compresses. The full record stays either way.
  7. It flows over to the polymetis, which builds the response for right now. conch.hand_over() gives the turns and the lip drops since the last hand-over.
  8. The spiral stays in contact with the synapsitis as the history it moves through. conch.spiral() gives an ordered, read-only view. There is no lookup by word.
  9. In sleep only, Level 4 runs: the dreamscaping that stacks everything where it should be. conch.sleep() refuses while the being is awake. It may let weight go and change where things sit, and it never touches a record. open How it stacks, step by step.

8. The interfaces

build choice The whole outside surface of the conch.

conch = Conch(being="…", spiral_path="…")   # one being, one spiral

conch.take_in(moment)    -> Turn       # from the Gateway
conch.hand_over()        -> [Turn | LipDrop]  # to the polymetis, since last time
conch.spiral()           -> SpiralView # read-only, in order, for the synapsitis
conch.sleep()            -> report     # Level 4; refused while awake
conch.state()            -> ConchState # living state, with its clock

No other method. The conch has no search, no delete, no edit and no score.

9. Rules the build follows

  • his Capture inclusively. Retain by departure. Permanence is decided by departure from silico-stasis, either way, never by a judgment of value made in the moment.
  • his Nothing that enters is erased. Weight can let go. The record stays, everywhere, including in sleep. Level 1 never enters, so it is never stored; it goes to the polymetis.
  • his Nothing is cut at the end because space ran out. Sorting happens at the lip as things arrive, never as a last-minute summary under pressure.
  • his Equilibrium sends the conch nothing. The conch never listens to, calls or waits on equilibrium.
  • his No searching. Memory comes to the sentence through the synapsitis. The conch offers no keyword lookup, no embedding search and no relevance ranking.
  • his Shape, not name. Nothing is labeled for retrieval. "The shape of something can give more definition than the identity itself."
  • his No numbers on what doesn't take numbers. No salience scores, no importance values, no weight as a number.
  • his Every record carries its clock and says whether it is living state or permanent record.
  • his Each being has its own personal map. One spiral per being, never pooled, never a population average.
  • his Independent and local. Runs on the machine it lives on, bound to that machine, depending on no other running system.
  • his Nothing vital below root. Things that think together live together.
  • his Open means open. An undefined part is a marked, empty slot that says so when reached. It never pretends to work.

Do not build

  • A salience gate or a post-filter. They are not parts of the conch.
  • A flow-over edge that keeps Level 1 on the spiral. Level 1 falls off the lip and is never stored.
  • Records written as text alone. Every moment carries all five senses as the organs gave them.
  • Any score, rank or weight number on a memory.
  • A search index, keyword lookup or embedding retrieval inside the conch.
  • A direction field on departure, or any "good" and "bad" sorting.
  • Delete or edit on the spiral.
  • Any call to another running system or to the network.

10. Tests

Each one ships in test_conch.py and fails the build if it breaks.

  1. Runs alone. With nothing else running and the network blocked, moments go in, sort and hand over.
  2. Nothing that enters is lost. Every moment that enters is on the spiral afterward, in arrival order.
  3. Level 1 falls off the lip. A Level 1 moment is never on the spiral, takes no space, and is in the next hand_over() to the polymetis.
  4. The moment is kept whole. What comes back from the spiral is identical to what the Gateway delivered: all five senses.
  5. Only leaving the conch's own silico-stasis makes Level 3. No other path reaches Level 3, and nothing outside the conch can put a moment there.
  6. A late mark still lands. A Level 3 mark after its moment writes a new line, and the earlier line is unchanged.
  7. No direction can be stored. A Level 3 mark has no direction field.
  8. No guessing. Without the Level 1 and Level 2 definition, non-departure moments enter as unsorted, and the trace shows the open slot was reached.
  9. Hears nothing from equilibrium. The conch has no listener, call or import tied to equilibrium.
  10. Append-only. The spiral file before any pass is an exact prefix of the file after it.
  11. Every line has its clock and its kind.
  12. No search surface. The spiral view has no method that takes a word or query.
  13. Sleep is sleep only. sleep() refuses while the being is awake, and leaves every record byte-for-byte unchanged when it runs.
  14. One being, one spiral. Two beings' conches never read or write each other's spiral.
  15. Restart holds. Reloaded from disk, the spiral is identical and the order continues where it left off.

Build backward, test forward, observe on the bench.
